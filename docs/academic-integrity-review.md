## Official findings

### Qualification and assessment method

Gateway lists the Level 5 Diploma in Web Application Development (`603/5574/8`), Unit 2 `A/650/3526`, as a portfolio-of-evidence qualification. The supplied excerpt contains the Unit 2 pass, merit and distinction criteria. [Gateway Qualifications: Level 5 Diploma in Web Application Development](https://www.gatewayqualifications.org.uk/qualification/gateway-qualifications-level-5-diploma-in-web-application-development/), retrieved 26 October 2026.

### AI and use in assessment principles

Gateway's principles apply to all its qualifications. They say assessment evidence must be authentic; centres must authenticate learner evidence; learners should submit a signed and dated authenticity declaration; and centres must tell learners what AI use is and is not permitted. AI-generated material reproduced in assessment evidence must be identified and referenced where its use is permitted. Improper AI use that subverts assessment is malpractice. [Gateway Qualifications: Artificial Intelligence and use in Assessment - Principles](https://www.gatewayqualifications.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Artificial-Intelligence-and-use-in-Assessment.pdf), retrieved 26 October 2026.

### Current malpractice and centre guidance

Gateway's current malpractice policy defines misuse of generative AI as using it to produce work that does not reflect the learner's own understanding or failing to acknowledge AI assistance. Its centre handbook says AI use that prevents a learner from independently demonstrating attainment is likely to be malpractice, and centres must make clear when AI is permitted, restricted or prohibited. [Gateway Qualifications: Malpractice and Maladministration Policy and Procedure](https://www.gatewayqualifications.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Malpractice-and-Maladministration-Policy-and-Procedure-version-8.0-July-2022.pdf) and [Gateway Qualifications Centre Handbook: Quality & Compliance](https://www.gatewayqualifications.org.uk/centre-support/quality-assurance/centre-handbook/quality-compliance/), retrieved 26 October 2026.

These public rules establish the authenticity risk and the centre's responsibilities. They do not provide this learner's centre-specific permission, assignment declaration form or remediation decision.
